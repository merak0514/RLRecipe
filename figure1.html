Source
Training run log, 2026-09-02 snapshot. Both arms start from the same checkpoint and are evaluated under the same protocol: AIME 2025, sampled every 5 steps.
Smoothing
Centered moving average over a window measured in training steps, so the 5-step eval cadence and the per-step rollout cadence are treated alike. The raw unsmoothed records are plotted alongside as faint dots; no noise is hidden. The figures load at ± 5 steps. Caption values are rounded so they hold at any setting — a peak read off a lightly smoothed curve is partly sampling noise and shrinks as the window widens.
Discard ratio
One definition across arms: GRPO arms use the all-zero plus all-one group share; PPO arms are reconstructed as (D₀+D₁)/(D₀+D₁+60). Effective gradient groups = 1 − the plotted value.
